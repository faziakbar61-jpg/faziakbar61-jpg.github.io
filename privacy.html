Doorway Draft Privacy Policy

A&S Interactive · Effective 29 October 2026

Doorway Draft is a puzzle game for Android made by A&S Interactive ("we"). This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it collect, why, and the choices you have. The game has no accounts and asks you for no personal details. We do not run our own servers and we do not sell your data.

Information kept on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, gems, settings, purchases you made and the current puzzle) is saved in a file on your phone. It is not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this file in your device backup.

Information collected by services in the game

The game uses the following Google services. They collect information directly from your device and process it under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

  • Google AdMob (advertising): the advertising ID, device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language and an approximate location derived from your IP address) and how you interact with ads. Used to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent): records the privacy choices you make in the consent form, where one is shown.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google): how the game is used, for example levels started and finished, rooms redrawn, screens opened and ads watched, together with device information, an app instance ID and the advertising ID. Used to understand how players use the game and to improve it. Events never contain your name, email, contacts or other personal details.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): crash reports and diagnostic information (device model, operating system version, the state of the app when it crashed). Used to find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): reads game settings, such as how often ads may appear, from Google's servers.
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases): Google processes all payments. We never see your card or payment details; the game only receives a record of what was bought so it can deliver the item.

The game can show a daily reminder notification if you allow it. Reminders are created on your phone and involve no data collection.

Sharing a result (for example a story card) uses Android's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form asks for your choice before ads load. You can change it any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options. If you decline analytics storage, the game turns analytics collection off.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Ads: rewarded ads are always optional. The "Remove Forced Ads" purchase removes all other ads.
  • Notifications: turn the daily reminder on or off in the game's Settings or in Android's app settings.

Children

Doorway Draft is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and want their data removed, contact us at the address below.

Keeping and deleting data

We hold no data ourselves. Information collected by the services above is kept by Google for the periods set out in its policies, and is protected in transit with encryption (HTTPS). To ask about or request deletion of data linked to your device, email us and we will help you through Google's tools. Uninstalling the game deletes the progress file on your phone.
